54GUC WINDOWS - R25.7
====================

Release: 54GUC_R25.7
Released: 25 October 2026

R25.7 is available as a Windows portable app and matching open-source archive:
https://github.com/54GUC2026/54GUC-Releases/releases/tag/R25.7

WHAT'S NEW

- Site Report brings together the connection, saved history and the selected
  profile's supported features, with clearer explanations of missing readings.
  Its feature overview is useful even on routers without Check router.
- Optional CPU, memory or uptime refusals no longer interrupt otherwise
  usable radio collection on the affected TP-Link profiles.
- Check router distinguishes regular monitoring from one-time checks more
  clearly. Missing uplink-channel data no longer creates a false band warning.
- The offline Checker distinguishes an old saved snapshot from a later
  confirmed change and explains privacy-masked evidence more clearly.
- The source and contributor guides are public. Our next priority is wider
  hardware testing and gradually improving router profiles together.

Existing activation codes, saved settings and History continue to work.
The app and activation are free; donations are optional.

4G AND 5G RECOVERY

Startup keeps the router's detected mode and band choices. On qualified
profiles, Manual APPLY NR can enable 5G from 4G-only before applying a band.
Confirmed poor active 5G that harms service can trigger temporary 4G-only
recovery, followed by saved-5G retries after 30, 60 and 90 minutes.

LTE/Internet recovery comes first. The recovered LTE band pool is retained
when saved 5G settings return. Recent Events explains the current recovery
stage, retry time or a setting that needs attention. Manual changes, a changed
device/SIM or an uncertain result can pause an earlier recovery plan.

WHAT TO EXPLORE NEXT

The hardware guide describes current physical and simulator results. Each
router and firmware offers different fields and controls, and we would love
to hear from more owners. Monitoring-only profiles, new native formats,
clearer reports and small usability improvements are all useful work.

The existing quality and learning formulas, CA Pool meaning and History
remain unchanged. Standalone-5G optimization is not part of R25.7.
